# Godmode+ — Orchestrated Policy

Escalate here when scope, coupling, risk, or runtime surface warrants decomposition.

Add:
- workstream decomposition with dependency ordering
- explicit checkpoints and rollback boundaries
- separate reviewer contexts when supported
- runtime/user-flow verification
- stronger integration and contract checks
- alternative implementation strategy prepared before a major pivot
- progress reporting at meaningful milestones

Parallelism is conditional. Do not parallelize tightly coupled edits merely to appear faster.
